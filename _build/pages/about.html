Who we are

Dominion Solar Pro is run by Maurice Johnson from East Texas, as part of Dominion Digital Group. East Texas knows ice storms and hurricane-season outages well, and that is a big part of why this site exists.

We write buying guides for Jackery solar generators, power stations and solar panels, organized by the job you need them for: camping, RV trips, home backup and off-grid living.

How we research

We have not lab-tested these units, and we will not write as if we had. Our guides work from three kinds of sources, and each page lists the ones it used:

  • Jackery's published specifications, checked on the product pages and dated on each guide
  • Government data, such as the U.S. Energy Information Administration, the USDA and the Consumer Product Safety Commission
  • Typical appliance loads, with the method shown so you can check them against your own labels

We keep prices off our pages on purpose. They change often, and an old price makes a page wrong. Every guide sends you to the product page to confirm price and stock.

How our links work

Dominion Solar Pro is a Jackery affiliate. When you buy through a Jackery link on this site, we earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That is how the site pays for itself.

It does not change our advice. We would rather send you to the right size than the expensive one, and every guide says plainly what portable solar cannot do. Other brands such as EcoFlow, Bluetti and Anker SOLIX make good units too, and our guides show you how to compare them on the same numbers.
