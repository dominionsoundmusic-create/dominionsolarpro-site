Jackery Solar Generator Lineup, Explained

Explorer, HomePower, Plus, v2, Ultra, Pro Max. Jackery's model names tell you a lot once you know the code. Here is what each one means, every current model side by side, and how to pick.

Bundle or power station alone?

Buy the Solar Generator bundle if you plan to use solar from the start, such as for camping, off-grid use or long outages. The bundled panels are matched to the unit's solar input.

Buy the power station alone if you mainly want outage backup charged from the wall. You can add SolarSaga panels later. Our solar panel guide shows how many you need.

Before you buy

Check the warranty line

Several Jackery product pages list a 5-year warranty when you buy directly from Jackery.com, such as the Explorer 1500 Ultra and HomePower 2000 Plus v2. Read the warranty line on the page you buy from.

Consider refurbished

Jackery.com sells refurbished units of several models at lower prices. Stock comes and goes.

Confirm the specs on the page

Jackery's own pages occasionally show two different figures for the same model. If a number matters to you, such as surge watts or solar input, confirm it on the product page or with Jackery support before you order.

Where Jackery is not the right answer

No portable unit, from Jackery or anyone else, runs central air conditioning, electric heat or a whole house through a long outage. If that is what you need, look at a permanent standby system or a home battery installed by a licensed contractor. EcoFlow, Bluetti and Anker SOLIX also make portable units; compare them on capacity, output, battery cycles and solar input.

Common questions

What is the difference between an Explorer and a Solar Generator?

An Explorer is the power station by itself. A Jackery Solar Generator is the same power station bundled with SolarSaga panels.

What does Plus mean?

On current models it generally marks units built to expand with add-on battery packs, such as the HomePower 2000 Plus v2 (up to 12 kWh) and the Explorer 5000 Plus (up to 30 kWh per unit).

Which Jackery is best for home backup?

For most homes, the HomePower 2000 Plus v2. For a fridge and freezer on a transfer switch, the HomePower 3000. For 240-volt loads, the Explorer 5000 Plus or HomePower 3600 Pro Max.

Is it better to buy from Jackery.com?

Several product pages list a longer warranty when you buy direct, and Jackery.com sells refurbished units. Check the warranty line before buying anywhere.
